Project & data

Databases and impact methods live in one library on this computer and are shared by all projects. A project file holds only your models, so it stays small and can be sent to a colleague.

        Goal & scope
        
      
        A model is one product system. Every amount in the inventory is per functional unit defined here (ISO 14044 4.2).

        Parameters 
        
        
        Use parameters in amounts (e.g. thickness * area * density). Project parameters apply to all models; model parameters override them. Give a distribution to include a parameter in Monte Carlo and Sobol analysis.
        
        
        Applies toNameValue or formulaUnitUncertaintyCurrent value
        
        
        
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
        
        
        
        Correlated parameters 
        
        
        When two uncertain parameters move together (e.g. fuel use and distance), give their Spearman rank correlation from -0.99 to 0.99. Monte Carlo then samples them together and keeps each parameter's own distribution. Correlations apply to the parameters of this model and the project.
        
        
          
        
          
          
          
          
        
          
      
          

      
          
        
          Scenarios 
          
        
          A scenario is a set of parameter values, e.g. "thick insulation" or "French electricity". Pick one in step 4 or compare them in step 5.

                Recycling with the Circular Footprint Formula
                
          
                PEF method (Commission Recommendation (EU) 2021/2279, Annex C). Cyclia turns one material into inventory lines: virgin and recycled input, recycling and energy recovery at end of life with their credits, and disposal. Use the A, B and Q values of your PEFCR or of Annex C of the PEF method.
                
          
                
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
            
          
                
          
                
          
                Datasets
                
          
                
          
                
        
                
        
                
          
                Inventory from a paper or table
                
          
                Paste a table copied from a PDF, Word or Excel (one row per line: name, amount, unit, optional stage), or choose a file (PDF, Word, Excel, CSV). Cyclia proposes a dataset or elementary flow for each row with a matching unit; you confirm every row before anything is added.
                
          
          
                
          
                
          
                
        
                
        
                
          
                Emissions from an external model
                
          
                Couple a fate, leaching, runoff or field-emission model: paste or choose its output as CSV with the columns year, flow, compartment, subcompartment, amount, unit. Each row becomes a direct emission per functional unit; the year feeds the dynamic climate analysis.

                      Validation suite
                      
          
                      Runs 35 small product systems with known exact answers (chains, loops, waste treatment, allocation, system expansion, nesting, units, regional and site factors, background scenarios, scenario files, module D, dynamic GWP, Monte Carlo, Sobol, fate model) in a temporary library and compares Cyclia with them. Attach the report to a paper or a thesis.

                      Background scenarios
                      
          
                      Change the background database before calculating, without editing it. Prospective: scale emissions or inputs of selected datasets towards a target year (linear between the base year and the years you give), in the spirit of premise. Consequential: replace the suppliers of a market by its marginal supplier. Choose a scenario in step 4.
                      
          
                        
        
                        
        
                        
          
                        Import a premise or Activity Browser scenario file
                        
          
                        Scenario difference files (Excel or CSV, one column of amounts per scenario) made with premise from integrated assessment models (e.g. REMIND or IMAGE, SSP pathways) or in the Activity Browser. Each scenario column becomes a background scenario; exchanges are matched to your library by dataset name, product and location, and elementary flows by name and compartment. Use the same database version the file was made for.

                        Consequential: marginal suppliers
                        
          
                        Lists the markets in the supply chain of the current model with their present suppliers. Pick the marginal supplier (the one that responds to a change in demand) for the markets that matter.
                        
          
                        
          
                        
        
                        
      
                        
      
                        
        
                        
          
                        Screening toxicity factors for your own chemicals
                        
          
                        For substances without official USEtox factors. A steady-state multimedia fate model (Mackay EQC Level III: air, water, soil, sediment) gives fate factors; USEtox-style exposure and effect factors turn them into freshwater ecotoxicity (CTUe) and human toxicity (CTUh) per kg emitted. Factors are saved as the method "Cyclia screening toxicity (own chemicals)" and the chemical becomes an elementary flow you can add in step 3. Use official USEtox factors for published results where they exist.

                          Site-specific characterisation factors
                          
          
                          Replace the generic factor of a flow by your own value (for example from a local fate model or measured bioavailability). It applies to the model's own direct emissions only, not to the background database, and is reported in the checks.
