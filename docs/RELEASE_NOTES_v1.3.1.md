Cyclia 1.3.1 makes the first step much clearer.

## What is new

- **You always see which project is open**: a card at the top of step 1 shows the open project, its models and when it was last saved, with Switch project and Continue.
- **One thing at a time**: projects, databases, impact methods and the software checks each have their own tab. Cyclia opens the tab of whatever is still missing.
- **Projects as cards**: click a card to open a project; the open one is clearly marked.
- **Tidier databases and methods**: each shows its version, system model and size at a glance; the import options appear only when you press "Add a database" or "Add methods".
- **Less clutter**: when a step is complete, its guidance card folds away.
- Citation and DOI updated in the app and in the reports.
