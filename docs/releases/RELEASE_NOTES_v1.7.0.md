# Cyclia 1.7.0

## What is new

- **Life cycle costing as ISO 15686-5 asks.** Residual value at the end of the study period, price escalation per
  cost type, and the uncertainty of prices as a range of the net present cost.
- **Material flows and environmental impacts together.** Charge production on virgin material only, give recycling
  a credit for the material it recovers, and see primary supply, use, recycling, losses and credits year by year.
- **Social LCA assessment.** Score the organisations of your supply chain per subcategory of the UNEP 2020
  guidelines, against a reference point, and see coverage, hotspots and what is still missing.
- **Allocation side by side.** The results of your model under every allocation choice in one table.
- **Factor packages checked.** When you import AWARE, LANCA or USEtox, Cyclia tells you whether the package is
  complete and consistent.
- **Validation in the report.** The report shows the validation evidence and the critical review checklist.

## Changed

- With a replacement interval and a study period, life cycle costs now include the residual value of the last
  purchase (ISO 15686-5). Switch it off in step 2 to get the earlier figures.

## Download

Windows: `Cyclia-Setup-1.7.0.exe` · macOS: `.dmg` · Linux: `.AppImage` or `.deb`.
