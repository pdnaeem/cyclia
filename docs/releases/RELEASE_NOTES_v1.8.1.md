# Cyclia 1.8.1

Corrections to 1.8.0; results of calculations are unchanged. The new features of 1.8.0 (supplier data requests,
critical review between two computers, optimisation of choices, Sobol indices with your own parameters, regional
factors with country fallback) are all included.

## Fixed

- An ecoinvent benchmark now appears in the Benchmark Centre. Before, it showed its report but stayed "Not run"
  there.
- Merging review files on Windows keeps the later change of a comment, also when two changes happen within the
  same clock tick.

## Download

Windows: `Cyclia-Setup-1.8.1.exe` · macOS: `.dmg` · Linux: `.AppImage` or `.deb`.
Installed copies of Cyclia update themselves.
