# Cyclia 1.6.2

## What is new

- **Scientific Benchmark Centre.** All the evidence for Cyclia's calculations in one table, with references,
  differences and a status that counts only checks that actually ran and passed. Includes a new IPCC 2021 check.
- **Uncertain parameters.** Give a parameter of your own data a range or distribution and the full Monte Carlo
  carries it through every formula that uses it.
- **Undo, versions and restore** for your processes, and a list of everything that depends on a process.
- **More allocation choices.** Physical causality, economic allocation with currency and price year, and a
  side-by-side comparison of allocation methods.
- **Edit your model in the diagram.** Move lines between stages, change amounts, mark credits and remove lines.
- **Full provenance.** Every dataset of your supply chain, with its database, version and contribution, in the
  Excel export and the report.

## Fixed

- After you changed the compartment of one of your own flows, its old characterisation factor could still be
  used. It is now held for your review.

- On some computers the top of the screen blinked faintly every few seconds. It no longer does.
- In a narrow window, the inventory and the background scenario editor no longer make the page scroll sideways.
- Long names in the supply chain diagram end with "…" instead of being cut mid-word.

## Download

Windows: `Cyclia-Setup-1.6.2.exe` · macOS: `.dmg` · Linux: `.AppImage` or `.deb`.
Installed copies of Cyclia update themselves.
