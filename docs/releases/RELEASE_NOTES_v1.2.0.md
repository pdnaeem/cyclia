# Cyclia 1.2.0: free, open-source LCA software for students and researchers

Cyclia is now free and open source under the MIT licence.

## Download

| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 | `Cyclia-Setup-1.2.0.exe` |
| macOS (Apple silicon and Intel) | `Cyclia-1.2.0-arm64.dmg` / `Cyclia-1.2.0-x64.dmg` |
| Linux | `Cyclia-1.2.0.AppImage` or `.deb` |

On first start choose **Learn with a first study** to try Cyclia with the built-in tutorial database.

## Highlights

- **Guided ISO 14040/14044 workflow**: every step explains what it means, gives an example, lists common
  mistakes and shows a checklist that ticks itself
- **ecoinvent with your own licence**: download inside the app, then validate Cyclia against ecoinvent's
  own LCIA results automatically
- **EF 3.1** with official JRC normalisation and weighting; ReCiPe 2016, CML-IA, IPCC 2021
- **Standards outputs**: EN 15804+A2 EPD, PEF most relevant results, ISO 14067, GHG Protocol
- **Prospective and consequential LCA** with background scenarios, including premise scenario files
- **Toxicity** characterisation for your own chemicals with a multimedia fate model
- **Uncertainty**: Monte Carlo with the pedigree matrix, sensitivity analysis and Sobol indices
- **Validation suite** of 34 product systems with exact answers, with a Word report for your thesis
- **Interoperability**: openLCA JSON-LD, SimaPro CSV, ILCD and ecoSpold import; Brightway and openLCA export;
  Python and R clients
- **New design**: welcome screen, dark mode, new logo

Full list in [docs/CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/pdnaeem/cyclia/blob/main/docs/CHANGELOG.md).

## Notes

- Installers are not code-signed yet. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC": click **More info**,
  then **Run anyway**. On macOS, right-click the app and choose **Open** the first time.
- Cyclia includes no database. The tutorial and demo data are illustrative and must not be published.
