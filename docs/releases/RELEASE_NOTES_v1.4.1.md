# Cyclia 1.4.1

## What is new

- Cyclia's results agree with ecoinvent's own published LCIA results: 100 % of 2,500 results within 0.1 %, median
  deviation 3.9e-07 % (EF v3.1, cut-off, 100 datasets from every group, 25 categories). Details:
  docs/VALIDATION_ECOINVENT.md.
- The benchmark report now names the database, its version and system model, the methods and the ecoinvent files it
  was compared with.

## Download

Windows: `Cyclia-Setup-1.4.1.exe` · macOS: `.dmg` · Linux: `.AppImage` or `.deb`.
Installed copies of Cyclia update themselves.
