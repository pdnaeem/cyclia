# Cyclia 1.6.0

## What is new

- **Custom data** (Toolbox > Custom data). Create your own databases, product flows, elementary flows, processes
  and characterisation factors, and build a complete supply chain with them, from an empty library or together
  with installed databases.
- **Inputs, waste and credits.** A process takes inputs from your own processes or from any installed dataset,
  sends waste to a treatment process, avoids products, and has emissions and resources, each with its unit,
  uncertainty or pedigree scores, and source.
- **Every item is traceable.** Unique identifier, source, author, dates and revision for every flow, process and
  factor.
- **Units are checked.** Enter 3.6 MJ for an electricity input in kWh or 10 g for an emission in kg and Cyclia
  converts it; an amount in an incompatible unit is refused.
- **No hidden zeros.** When one of your flows has no factor in an impact category, the result of that category
  is marked incomplete and the flow is listed, until you give the factor or record that the category does not
  apply.
- **Check and share.** A database check lists everything that is missing, and a custom database can be exported
  and imported as one JSON file.

## Download

Windows: `Cyclia-Setup-1.6.0.exe` · macOS: `.dmg` · Linux: `.AppImage` or `.deb`.
Installed copies of Cyclia update themselves.
