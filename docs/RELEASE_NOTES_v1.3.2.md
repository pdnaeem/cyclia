# Cyclia 1.3.2

Cyclia 1.3.2 is the largest update so far. It helps you interpret results, document your data and write
the reports reviewers and clients ask for.

## What is new

**Understand your result**
- **Summary tab with key findings.** After you calculate, Cyclia explains what drives each result, where the
  hotspots differ between categories (burden shifting), which change would lower the result most, and which
  important lines still rest on weak data.
- **Data quality of the results**: a data quality rating per impact category (PEF form) and the share of
  primary data.
- **Compare** tells you which alternative is lowest in each category and warns when differences are small.

**Document your data**
- Mark each inventory line as **primary, secondary or estimate**.
- **Details** per line: reference, year of the data, supplier or site, third-party checked, and a price.
  Data older than 10 years raise a warning.

**New analyses**
- **Life cycle costing** with discounting.
- **Circular Footprint Formula** of the EU PEF method for recycled content, recycling and energy recovery.
- **Water inventory**: withdrawal, release and consumption.
- **Correlated parameters** and a **convergence check** for Monte Carlo.
- **Database versions**: compare two ecoinvent versions and move a model to the new one with one click
  (and undo).
- **Project overview** of all models.

**Reports**
- **Report centre**: see what each report still needs.
- **Executive summary** (two pages) and **figures for journals** (PNG at 300 dpi and SVG).
- **Review and verification status** printed in the report.
- **EPD updated to ISO 14025:2026** (published June 2026).
- Modelling approach (attributional or consequential) and technological coverage in the scope, with checks.

Results of existing models do not change. Recalculate to see the new Summary tab.
